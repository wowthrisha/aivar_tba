## Item 0 — Theoretical reframing (25 seconds, insert at 2:00)

> "One framing point. This is a deferral policy — the formalism goes
> back to Chow's reject option in 1970, through selective
> classification, to what's now called learning to defer.
>
> My rejector is deterministic rather than learned, and that's
> deliberate: learned deferral needs human decisions for every training
> instance, which don't exist on day one, and a black-box rejector
> inside a human-oversight system defeats its own purpose.
>
> The known limitation is that I defer on action risk, not on modelled
> human competence — which is exactly what the oversight metrics
> endpoint starts to measure."
